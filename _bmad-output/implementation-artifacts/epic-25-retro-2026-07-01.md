## What went well

- **Byte estimates accurate-to-conservative, every story.** Every story landed inside
  its band; 25.3 and 25.8 hit exactly; only 25.2 came in materially *under*
  (over-estimated the yielding-thread rewrite). Zero silent overshoots. The B.2
  per-component itemisation + ×1.25 register-juggle margin held up well.
- **The `(DELAY)` seam pre-engineered in Story 24.2 paid off exactly as designed** —
  25.3 was *one thread cell* (`DW w_PAUSE_cf`), +2 B to the byte. The Epic 24 retro's
  "build the foundation for the epic after next" lesson cashing in.
- **PAUSE-as-thread-cell** (25.2's load-bearing discovery): yielding words compose PAUSE
  as a thread cell rather than `CALL`ing it (PAUSE ends in NEXT, can't be CALLed from a
  DEFCODE leaf). That single insight made 25.3 trivial and gave `KEY`/`(DELAY)` free
  break-inheritance in 25.7.
- **Reuse compounded.** `.throw_uncaught` + `task N:` notice (25.4's defensive fix →
  25.6) reused *verbatim* by 25.7 (`Ctrl-\` → `THROW -28` → same handler). `.BANKS` →
  `.TASKS`; the TAPE=12121 round-robin witness reused across 25.1/25.4/25.5/25.6/25.7.
- **Reviews well-calibrated.** Real bugs caught where they existed (25.1's two, 25.7's
  latch-ambush); the one deep clean pass (25.3, two independent finder agents / 14 axes)
  correctly reported *clean* rather than manufacturing findings. 25.5's deliberate-bug
  discrimination probing proved two plausible-looking "bugs" were non-issues masked by
  design.

## What was hard / recurring struggles

1. **The `TASK ( -- task )` handle footgun** — pinned in 25.1, then bit 25.6 directly
   (`' BOOM2 TASK CONSTANT BMT2` missing `DUP` → underflow → cooperative-deadlock hang).
   Recurs as the `TASK DUP CONSTANT` idiom in 25.4/25.6/25.7/25.8.
2. **`AGAIN` is undefined in antforth** — surfaced 25.4, re-confirmed on silicon in 25.7,
   a core rewrite driver in 25.8. Forever-loop form is `BEGIN … 0 UNTIL`.
3. **The two 25.1 register/stack gotchas became a standing contract** (preserve DE=IP;
   sp_base is per-task) — re-cited in five later stories. Captured in
   `project_multitasker_pause_register_contract`.
4. **Emulator-vs-silicon gap was the epic's defining tension** (6 of 8 stories). iz-cpm
   doesn't model the 0xFDC7 ISR → `TICKS` frozen → nonzero DELAY never completes under
   emulation; non-interactive pipe → input always ready → live-prompt responsiveness
   provable only on hardware. Every story stated the split honestly. 25.2's "background
   counter advanced ~14,426 while operator idled" is the canonical
   can-only-happen-on-silicon witness. 25.7 *broke* the pattern — in-band `Ctrl-\` means
   the emulator drives the real 0x1C byte end-to-end.

## Next epic: Epic 26 — Inter-Task Coordination (Semaphores)

3 stories: **26.1** counting semaphore (`SIGNAL`/`WAIT`), **26.2** mutex (binary
semaphore, built on 26.1), **26.3** one-slot mailbox — all cooperative spin-with-`PAUSE`,
non-atomic by design, in `src/multitasker.asm`'s coordination section (FR17/18/19).

**Readiness: green.** Every Epic 26 precondition ("given a working scheduler") is
delivered and silicon-verified. `PAUSE`, the TCB ring, TASK/ACTIVATE — all shipped. No
plan invalidation, no dependency gaps.

**Three Epic-25 discoveries shape Epic 26 acceptance (flag, not re-plan):**

1. **Confirmed-on-silicon deadlock/starvation reality.** No-AWAKE-task → PAUSE walk hangs;
   a non-yielding lock-holder = reset-required (proven with `HOG` in 25.7). Epic 26's
   `WAIT`/mutex-lock are *exactly* "spin with PAUSE until condition" → their deadlock
   semantics must be an explicit, probed AC, not just "no deadlock in the happy path."
2. **`TASK ( -- task )` handle footgun + `AGAIN`-undefined will bite Epic 26 authors**
   (semaphore constructor words, demo loops). Cheap to pre-empt by citing the standing
   contract in the 26.1 story context.
3. **Interactive cursor-desync** (25.4) is largely dodged — Epic 26 probes are
   producer/consumer over buffers, less interactive — but any Epic 26 *demo* inherits it.

**Significant-discovery check: no plan-level surprise.** The one forward-coupling —
cooperative deadlock is real and reset-required on silicon — *sharpens* Epic 26's
semaphore ACs (AI-25-3) rather than changing the plan. **No epic-planning-review session
required before Epic 26.**

## Known limitations / carried debt (net, epic-end)

- Interactive line-editor cursor desync vs. a chatty background task (25.4) —
  display-desync only, not buffer corruption; real console-output coordination is future.
- Cooperative deadlock (no AWAKE task) and non-yielding-task stall = reset-required —
  documented-not-guarded by design (FR22); confirmed unrecoverable on silicon (25.7 `HOG`).
- DELAY accuracy degrades under pathological multitask load — inherent to cooperative
  scheduling, documented.
- Stale-handle hazards (FORGET/MARKER past an active TCB; a task whose bank was torn
  down) — documented-not-guarded across 25.1/25.4/25.5.
