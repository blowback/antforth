## What went well

1. **Sound-design-over-spec-sketch, twice.** Both feature stories found their *own recommended approach unsound at dev-pass and re-architected it correctly.* The 21.2 case is the load-bearing one: the spec's `DROP DUP EXECUTE QSAVE` thread shape corrupts `BANK!`'s argument (`w_EXECUTE_cf` pops its TOS from *below* the xt), and the return-stack-stash fallback is *also* unsound — an interactively-typed `>R`/`R>` rearranges the R-stack mid-EXECUTE (caught as a real self-introduced 20-2/20-3 regression: `result=2593`). The fix — **peek/flag/commit via a UserArea flag immune to both stacks** (`w_QMARK_BANK_cf` / `w_QSAVE_BANK_cf`) — is exactly the `project_interp_execute_no_stash_across_execute` lesson, rediscovered the hard way and now load-bearing.
2. **CR found the silent path again — every pass.** The `source_id==0` fix closes a leak the happy path never reaches: `include_top` only excludes INCLUDE, but EVALUATE sets `source_id=-1` without bumping `include_top`. Adversarial review stays load-bearing, not ceremony (continues the 19.5 → 20 → 21 streak; the find is consistently a silent-corruption path).
3. **Epic 20's envelope prediction was validated.** Action A2 said "Epic 21 is pure-addition, no substitution → revert to the ~2.4× multiplier." +191 B landed at ~1.27× of the ~150 B target — comfortably inside. The "substitution-voids-the-multiplier" carve-out (Epic 20's hard lesson) correctly *did not* fire.
4. **Version-slug drift caught at DRAFT, not dev-pass.** Action A4 predicted the "slug 3.x.5 but tag v3.0.6" off-by-one. Story 21.3 folded it into the ACs before dev-pass started — one better than 20.3 (which caught it at draft too, but 21.3 inherited the prediction).
5. **Test-infra discipline held without re-litigation.** Isolated fixtures for every behavioural bank-switch probe; the `'`-not-`FIND` forgotten-word workaround (`FIND` still resolves a forgotten word — pre-existing quirk); banked-MARKER home-bank-invoke rule (cross-bank invoke hangs — no dispatch stub); TIB≤128; 0x1A EOF for SLIDE transfer. All applied as standing practice.

## What didn't — challenges

1. **The per-story AC9 byte lines were optimistic.** 21.1 = +82 B (line ~80, marginal); 21.2 = +109 B (line ~70 — **56% over**). Both within the *epic* multiplier, but the per-story estimates were anchored to a design that didn't survive contact: the sound 21.2 design needed *three* headerless words + a flag where the unsound sketch budgeted one `QSAVE`. **Ant's disposition: multiplier-absorbed noise, not an action item** — the epic envelope is the meaningful gate, not the per-story estimate.
2. **Baseline-size discrepancy recurred.** The Epic 20 retro reported 27625 B at v3.0.5; 21.1's clean rebuild measured 27888 B — a **263 B gap** — plus a 10 B inter-story drift (27970 ↔ 27960). The "re-`wc -c` from a clean rebuild" discipline caught it each time, but story-text byte figures keep drifting from the artifact (same smell flagged in Epic 20). The discipline works; the drift persists.
3. **A test-sequencing artifact in the 21.2 HW batch.** AC6(b) was typed while the live bank was still 5 (left over from probe a), so the inner THROW fired as `-273` not the intended `999 THROW`. The verdict (anti-stranding, `BANK@`=0) held regardless, but it's a reminder that hand-typed HW batches carry hidden state line-to-line.

## Key takeaways

1. **Stability is a measurable outcome, not a mood.** 4-for-4 prior-action follow-through, one CR defect (the predicted silent path), zero regressions, full HW ledger, envelope inside the multiplier. The boring retro is the good retro — especially entering the final epic.
2. **The multiplier holds for pure addition; the carve-out is substitution-only.** Epic 20's +680 B / 3.4× was a design substitution (multiplier void); Epic 21's +191 B / 1.27× is pure-addition (multiplier applies). The distinction predicted Epic 21 correctly — carry it to Epic 22's ~100 B / ~240 B envelope.
3. **Thread-shape spec sketches need dev-pass scrutiny.** Twice running, the as-written mechanism was unsound (EXECUTE stack-stash; bank-removal scrub) and the dev-pass re-architected it. Spec richly, but treat thread-shape sketches as hypotheses, not recipes — `project_interp_execute_no_stash_across_execute` is the standing lesson.
4. **A "closed" decision can still hide an open sub-question.** §9.1 was closed (fixed-memory-only) yet the *announcement policy* for 22.3 was genuinely undecided and the epics-file AC was stale against the binding doc. Re-validate inherited "§9.x closure" inputs against the actual PD-block at story draft, not just the epic prose.
5. **The slug is never the version.** `3.x.N` placeholders drift; the +1 downstream shift carries into Epic 22 (slug 3.x.6 → tag v3.0.7). Re-validate the version surface — all four surfaces — from source-of-truth at close-out.
