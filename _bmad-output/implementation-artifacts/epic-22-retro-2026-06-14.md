## Headline

Epic 22 is the **reflection + UX layer** on top of the banking mechanism — it adds no new banking *mechanism*, it makes the mechanism *legible*: `.BANKS` reports real per-bank `used`/`free`, the REPL prompt shows which bank new definitions land in, the cross-bank pointer hazards are documented for users, and `CODE` words are given a definite cross-bank disposition (silent redirect to fixed memory). Three pure-addition feature stories (+450 B aggregate) + a 0 B close-out. Every story HW-UAT-PASSed on silicon at its own close. The epic closes Phase 4.

## What went well

- **Per-story HW UAT cadence held (S9).** Each of 22.1/22.2/22.3 carried its own silicon PASS before status→done — no deferred-smoke debt accumulated into the close-out beyond the single consolidated final batch.
- **The +450 B was never a surprise.** Because each delta was itemised and accepted at its own close, the close-out reconciliation is a *summation*, not a *defence*. A2's "plan Epic 22 at ~2.4×" guidance was the right framing even though the actual came in at ~1.9× — pure-addition, dominated by 22.1's genuine per-bank computation.
- **22.3 closed a year-old ambiguity cleanly.** The §9.1 "can CODE words live in banks?" question (open since the redesign) resolved to a definite, testable contract (silent redirect to fixed memory; FIND page-in is address-conditioned, so fixed-addr CODE stays cross-bank-findable). The adversarial CR refuted both speculative triple-corruption candidates empirically — the [[project_bank_triple_excludes_buckets]] lesson paid off.
- **Reflection words exposed no mechanism bugs.** `.BANKS` walking real per-bank dictionaries surfaced no latent triple/allocator inconsistency — a good signal the Epic 19–21 lifecycle work was sound.

## What didn't — challenges

- **22.1's +282 B was the single biggest per-story delta of the epic** — real per-bank `used`/`free` computation is genuinely more code than a placeholder. Accepted, but it's the reason the epic ran ~1.9× over the nominal envelope.
- **`docs/banking-pointer-hazards.md` is invisible to `check-doc-sync`** (22.2 finding (6)). The user-docs surface has no drift gate; "clean" there means only "README link resolves." A standing gap, not new.
- **Banner-bump 0 B precedent makes silicon re-verification feel optional** — but the consolidated Surface-3 batch is still the right discipline; the v3.0.7 binary differs from the 22.3-tested one only by one banner character.

## Recurring review themes

- **Empirical refutation over speculative findings.** Both 22.3 CR candidates were killed by running the code, not by argument — consistent with the [[feedback_adversarial_review]] + [[project_bank_triple_excludes_buckets]] discipline (the per-bank triple excludes the shared bucket array, so most "triple corruption" findings are benign).
- **Long human-readable word names cost bytes but earn their keep** (22.2 `PROMPT-SHOW-BANK`). Accepted as a deliberate legibility trade.
