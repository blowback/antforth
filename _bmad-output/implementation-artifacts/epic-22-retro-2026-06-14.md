## Headline

Epic 22 is the **reflection + UX layer** on top of the banking mechanism — it adds no new banking *mechanism*, it makes the mechanism *legible*: `.BANKS` reports real per-bank `used`/`free`, the REPL prompt optionally shows which bank new definitions land in, the cross-bank pointer hazards are documented for users, and `CODE` words get a definite cross-bank disposition (silent redirect to fixed memory). Three pure-addition feature stories (**+450 B** aggregate) + a 0 B close-out. Every story HW-UAT-PASSed on silicon at its own close.

**Ant's verdict: "a good, easy finish to a rocky [phase]."** That is the right framing and the records back it — the *phase* was rocky (the Epic-19.5 stabilization interlude existing at all is the tell: DTC-dispatch rework, portal-aliasing guards, the sentinel trampoline abolished mid-flight for the RST-$28 self-dispatching stub). A clean, additive, every-story-green-on-silicon epic is exactly the soft landing a rocky phase wants to end on.

## What went well

1. **Per-story HW UAT cadence held (S9).** Each of 22.1/22.2/22.3 carried its own silicon PASS before status→done — no deferred-smoke debt accumulated into the close-out beyond the single consolidated final batch on the v3.0.7 binary.
2. **The +450 B was never a surprise.** Because each delta was itemised and accepted at its own close, the 22.4 reconciliation is a *summation*, not a *defence*. A2's "plan Epic 22 at ~2.4×" framing was right even though the actual came in at ~1.9× — pure-addition, dominated by 22.1's genuine per-bank computation.
3. **22.3 closed a long-standing §9.1 ambiguity cleanly.** "Can CODE words live in banks?" (open since the redesign) resolved to a definite, testable contract: silent redirect to fixed memory; FIND page-in is address-conditioned (`addr < $8000` → fixed), so a fixed-addr CODE word stays cross-bank-findable regardless of the fat bank byte. The [[project_bank_triple_excludes_buckets]] lesson paid off directly — the adversarial CR refuted **both** speculative triple-corruption candidates *empirically* (HERE rolled back, LATEST stable, words findable), and one proposed "fix" was net-harmful (10 B leak). One survivor: a comment-accuracy fix, binary byte-identical.
4. **Reflection words exposed zero latent mechanism bugs.** `.BANKS` walking real per-bank dictionaries surfaced no triple/allocator inconsistency — a strong signal the Epic 19–21 lifecycle work was sound. The reflection layer became, incidentally, a verification layer.
5. **The mechanism-election pattern matured.** 22.3's dev-pass found its *own* stated mechanism ("swap the triple only, keep `current_bank`=N") insufficient — `build_header` keyed layout + fat-pointer bank byte off `current_bank`, not the triple — surfaced two correct alternatives, and the project lead chose `triple_owner` (a 0-byte, behaviour-preserving change everywhere except the redirect window). Same "spec-sketch-as-hypothesis-not-recipe" discipline that paid off across Epics 20/21.

## What didn't — challenges

1. **22.1's +282 B was the single biggest per-story delta of the epic.** Real per-bank `used`/`free` is genuinely more code than a `0 / 16384` placeholder — a hand-rolled forced-decimal width-6 32-bit printer (~82 B, unavoidable under Q1=force-decimal since the BASE-respecting `D.R` can't be reused) plus the bank-0/bank-N branch and the live-vs-saved HERE read. Accepted, but it's why the epic ran ~1.9× over nominal.
2. **22.2 exposed a shared-fixture layout-fragility — flagged, not discharged.** Legitimate +132 B kernel growth pushed `kernel_end` past $8000, so the main suite's `_dot-banks-probe-y` ran its colon-body `BANK!` from window-resident code and tripped the Story-19.5.1 portal guard (`-273`). Fixed minimally (switch interactively / one-line `1 BANK! .BANKS`), but **this class will recur for any future kernel-growing story** ([[feedback_phase4_probe_bank_switch_limitation]]). Carry-forward: the main banking suite's bank-switching probes are a standing fragility as the kernel approaches $8000.
3. **`docs/*.md` has no drift gate.** `check-doc-sync`'s 7-file hardcoded registry never globs `docs/`, so 22.2's `docs/banking-pointer-hazards.md` is invisible to it — "clean" means only "the README link resolves." A standing process gap (low priority; see AI-22-3).
4. **Banner-bump-is-0 B makes silicon re-verification *feel* optional.** It isn't — the consolidated Surface-3 batch is the right discipline even when the v3.0.7 binary differs from the 22.3-tested one by one banner character. (And the Makefile test-80 banner assertion — A3's predicted "fourth surface" — *did* bite: the un-bumped assertion aborted `test-repl` early, caught only by the Step-9 re-run-on-final-binary discipline.)

## Recurring review themes

- **Empirical refutation over speculative findings.** Both 22.3 CR candidates were killed by *running the code*, not by argument — consistent with [[feedback_adversarial_review]] + [[project_bank_triple_excludes_buckets]] (the per-bank triple excludes the shared bucket array, so swaps never affect findability and most "triple corruption" findings are benign).
- **CR stays load-bearing — a dry pass is the exception, not the trend (project-lead call).** Epic 22's CR found nothing real, breaking a long streak (19.5.1 ×3, 19.5.2, Epic 20 bucket-scrub, Epic 21 EVALUATE-leak — each a silent-corruption path the happy path can't reach). **Ant's verdict: keep it cranked. "No findings is exceedingly rare; it usually catches something, often serious."** Read the dry pass as *this particular reflection epic had little attack surface + the mechanism has settled*, NOT as licence to ease adversarial intensity. Phase 5 inherits full-intensity CR.
- **Long human-readable word names cost bytes but earn their keep.** 22.2's `PROMPT-SHOW-BANK` (16 chars) + `(BANK-PROMPT)` (13 chars) = 29 B of name text alone — a deliberate legibility trade, accepted.
- **Itemise-and-accept-at-each-close → the epic close sums, never re-litigates.** The single most reusable process habit of Phase 4's back half.

## Phase-4 reflection (the close — Ant's verdict)

This is the final epic of the banking phase, so the Epic-22 lens widens to the whole bet.

**The banked-RAM bet delivered — project lead's verdict: "yes, spot on."** Phase 4 was the first *feature* phase since v2.0, and it shipped the promise: banked RAM enablement, end-to-end, observable on silicon. Cross-bank `:`, `CREATE`/`DOES>`, `EXECUTE`, THROW/ABORT restore, `MARKER`/`FORGET` per-bank lifecycle, bank-aware `FIND`/`WORDS`, and a reflection surface (`.BANKS`, prompt indicator) — all working on real MicroBeast at v3.0.7. Nothing the project lead would re-architect starting over.

**What the phase cost in stability, and how it was bought back.** The rocky middle — the Epic-19.5 interlude — was the price of getting cross-bank dispatch right: the sentinel-trampoline design was fragile (portal-window dictionary aliasing, not the emulator, was the hang class — [[feedback_iz_cpm_trampoline_fragility]] superseded) and was replaced by the RST-$28 self-dispatching stub (0 T-states/NEXT, DR-2). Framing that as an explicit *stabilization interlude epic* rather than smuggling it into a feature epic ([[feedback_stabilisation_interlude]]) was the right structural call — it owns its own retro and its own tag (v3.0.4).

**The standing lessons that held all phase:**
- **The per-bank triple excludes the shared fat bucket array** ([[project_bank_triple_excludes_buckets]]) — the single most load-bearing banking invariant; it's why findability composes and why "triple corruption" CR findings are usually benign. Re-confirmed empirically twice in 22.3 alone.
- **BIOS MBB page routines over direct MMU port I/O** ([[project_banking_bios_pivot]]) — the mid-phase pivot that made banking survive interrupts and re-paging; NFR-P4-3 re-baselined ≤400 T + MMU write (BANK! measured ~425 T).
- **Isolated fixtures for every non-zero-bank behavioural probe** ([[feedback_phase4_probe_bank_switch_limitation]]) — held as standing practice every epic; the one place it leaked (22.2 probe-y) was kernel-growth-induced, not discipline failure.
- **No accept-disposition for bugs; itemise scope costs honestly** ([[feedback_no_preexisting_discharge]]) — the +450 B is a legitimate, itemised, project-lead-visible *scope* cost, not a defect-accept.

**Envelope reality, codified.** Phase-4 cumulative **+3,504 B** (24,995 → 28,499) — well inside NFR-P4-5's ~6 KB @12 banks. Banking-infra total = 3,504 B code + 2,048 B reserved CCP-region structures = 5,552 B, inside the 8 KB @28-bank cap. The empirical **~2.4× multiplier for pure-addition** (with the *substitution-voids-the-multiplier* carve-out from Epic 20's +680 B/3.4× fat-pointer migration) is the Phase-4 budgeting lesson worth carrying to Phase 5 ([[project_epic17_envelope]]).

## Key takeaways

1. **A reflection/UX epic's value is legibility, not new mechanism — and its byte cost is honest feature code, not waste.** The biggest delta (22.1's +282 B) is the real per-bank computation `.BANKS` always promised.
2. **Itemise and accept binary deltas at each story's own close; the epic close-out then sums rather than re-litigates.** The single most reusable Phase-4 process habit.
3. **A dry CR pass is the rare exception, not a trend — keep adversarial review cranked.** Epic 22 broke a long silent-corruption-find streak only because a settled mechanism + a reflection epic gave it little to attack. CR usually catches something serious; Phase 5 inherits full intensity.
4. **Refute speculative review findings empirically — especially on the banking triple**, where the shared bucket array makes most "corruption" findings benign ([[project_bank_triple_excludes_buckets]]).
5. **A good, easy finish redeems a rocky phase.** The banked-RAM bet delivered (project lead: "spot on"); the rocky middle (Epic-19.5 interlude) was the honest price of getting cross-bank dispatch right, and framing it as its own interlude epic was the right structural call.
