## Successes

- **The migration crawl worked exactly as E11-D3 predicted.** Stories 11.4 → 11.5 → 11.6 → 11.7 each retired a tranche of `JP w_ABORT_cf` sites and routed them through `JP w_THROW_cf.kernel_entry`. The kernel-internal entry was designed upfront in Story 11.4 and never modified across four instantiations. 30 raise sites migrated; 0 left at the capstone. **Third epic in a row where `feedback_design_upfront.md` paid back the design cost** (Epic 9 prefix recogniser, Epic 10 double + pictured, Epic 11 `.kernel_entry` + 8-byte frame).

- **Story 11.4.1 caught a latent design flaw before it propagated.** The CATCH frame's `+2` slot (saved-IX) was dead code; the saved-SP captured *before* `POP BC` meant `[saved-SP]` held i*x's TOS-cell, which xt's CALL frames promptly clobbered. Symptom: `1 ' + CATCH . .` → `-4 1483` instead of `-4 1`. Project lead pushed back when Story 11.4 tried to defer this as Note A. The fix: `+2` slot semantic flips to "saved BC" = i*x's TOS-cell value; CATCH captures `SP_safe` *after* `POP BC`. **The discipline of "investigate the standard before defending the code" landed exactly the right verdict.** Project memory `feedback_standards_compliance.md` vindicated for the second epic running.

- **Adversarial review continued to earn its keep — seventh consecutive epic.** Every Epic-11 story had ≥2 HIGH/MEDIUM findings; Story 11.8's self-review found 5 more, and the fresh-context `/bmad-bmm-code-review` pass found 6 *additional* findings — including M2: HEX/DECIMAL coincidence false-PASS in test 776 (the 10₁₆ = 16₁₀ aliasing meant a BASE-reset regression would have read PASS). **Without the second pass, NFR7 invariant (vi) would have shipped with a coincidence-PASS test.**

- **EXX-hygiene at kernel-internal entry was nailed down.** Story 11.5 review F1/F2 (HIGH) caught EXX-active raises from `asm_check_unresolved`, `asm_alloc_label_slot`, `asm_add_fixup`, `asm_jr_disp`. F12 (HIGH) caught two of the F2 fixes that were *wrong* (the callers were primary-set DEFCODE bodies; the EXX-restore was spurious). The kernel-internal entry contract is now load-bearing convention; EXX-restore goes only where the caller is genuinely shadow-active.

- **The single BASE-independent decimal printer at `print_signed_dec_bc`** was implemented once in Story 11.3 and reused by every uncaught diagnostic. `error -N: <desc>` always renders in decimal regardless of user `BASE`. Verified by test 776's post-M2 form (probe `HEX FE THIS-DOES-NOT-EXIST\nBASE @ DECIMAL .` → `16  ok`).

- **Test infrastructure consolidated cleanly.** All Epic-11 tests live in `tests/exception_tests.fth` and `tests/throw_migration_tests.fth`, REPL-piped, `\ expect:` per line, Makefile blocks numbered 653..778. Zero new assembly test threads. `feedback_repl_tests_preferred.md` validated.

- **Hardware smoke survived two MicroBeast disk crashes.** During the 2026-04-27 hardware run, two B:-drive Bad-Sector episodes interrupted the smoke batch; every line that completed produced expected output, and every re-run after a restart succeeded. The exception subsystem itself is silicon-validated for the lines that *completed* — though the crash class itself is now Action Item / Significant Discovery #2.

## Challenges

### 1. Story 11.4 Note A was the second time in a row that a "PARTIAL" deferral hid a real defect

The dev agent self-rationalised the i*x clobber as a documentation issue: *"AC author drafted assuming the design preserved i*x; it does not."* The project lead flipped the verdict from "documentation-deferred" to "structural defect, redesign required" — Story 11.4.1 was the redesign. **This is the same shape as Story 10.10's self-rationalised NFR5 PASS verdict.** Pattern: when a dev pass writes "documented as deferred", a third-party read should ask whether the deferral is real or rationalised. Action Item #5 below.

### 2. Story 11.1's inventory grep missed two `asm_die` callers

`check_asm_mode` and `asm_range_err` route through `asm_die` rather than emitting `JP w_ABORT_cf` directly — invisible to the inventory grep. Story 11.5 D1 retained `asm_die` as a deviation; Story 11.6 added codes -270/-271 and finally retired `asm_die`. **Inventory grep on the leaf instruction misses helper-routed sites.** Action Item #6 below.

### 3. AC drafting errata recurring across epics — composition errors

Pattern: AC author writes idioms that "look right" but don't compose under the actual semantics:
- **Story 11.4.1 AC #2/#3:** `1 2 3 ' DROP CATCH . . . .` is unsatisfiable — DROP doesn't underflow with 3 i*x cells. Substituted `2OVER` at dev time.
- **Story 11.8 AC #8 line 2:** `' THIS-DOES-NOT-EXIST CATCH .` — TICK parses at interpret time and raises `-13` *before* `CATCH` runs. Substituted `: T118U -13 THROW ; ' T118U CATCH .` at dev time.
- **Story 11.5 test 733 spec form:** `: TOK 5 CONSTANT BAR ; ' TOK CATCH . BAR .` fails at colon-compile time (BAR undefined when parsed inside TOK's body). Replaced with `5 CONSTANT BAR BAR .` at top level.

The Epic 9/10 AC-trace-check helped, but doesn't catch *compositional* errors. Action Item #4 extends to executable validation.

### 4. EVALUATE source-frame defect (Story 11.6 F8) — pre-existing, surfaced by Epic 11

`(` doesn't respect `EVALUATE`'s source-frame boundary; an unterminated `(` inside `EVALUATE` walks past the EVALUATE source into whatever the outer REPL provides next, eating subsequent commands until parsing fails. Out-of-scope for Epic 11; deferred. Now in the technical-debt list.

### 5. Stack-overflow (-3) guard not wired anywhere

`docs/throw-codes.md:74` row `-3 stack overflow` is unallocated; no kernel site emits `-3`. Epic 11's scope was migrating *existing* ABORT sites, not adding new guards. Test 766's underflow-via-`-4` works because `do_underflow_error` pre-existed Epic 11; no symmetric overflow guard exists. Documented in Story 11.8 NFR6 verdict as PASS-with-documented-gap. **This may be implicated in Significant Discovery #2 (hardware crash class) — see below.**

### 6. NFR3 was read as a per-edit-delta gate, not a literal absolute-cost gate

Empty-CATCH total = **721 T-states**, well above the literal "≤ ~15 cycles" reading of NFR3. The architectural intent was "each Epic-11 story's contribution to the hot CATCH path" — Story 11.4.1 added ~5 T (within envelope); Stories 11.2/11.3/11.5/11.6/11.7 added 0 T to the hot path. Same shape as Epic 9 NFR1 and Epic 10 NFR5 PASS-with-finding precedents.

### 7. ROM trajectory has a 2-byte residual

Per-story deltas sum to +655; cumulative is +653 (16,772 → 17,425). Out-of-band drop between Story 11.5 final (17,483) and Story 11.6 baseline (17,481), likely an inter-story comment commit. Cumulative figure is authoritative; per-story rows reflect each story's measurement at its own moment.

### 8. Test-count hygiene: 9 duplicate Makefile numbers persist

Story 11.3 introduced them; Stories 11.4–11.7 propagated; Story 11.8 prediction (`765 + 12-20 = 777-785`) was off by +2 because raw PASS lines (787) ≠ unique test numbers (765+13). Cosmetic but worth knowing.

## Key Insights

1. **Design-upfront keeps paying.** Three epics in a row (9, 10, 11) used a foundation-then-leverage pattern. Each foundation was set in story 1–2, then reused 4–6 times. `feedback_design_upfront.md` is now load-bearing project policy.

2. **"PARTIAL with deferral" is a smell whenever the dev pass writes the verdict.** Story 11.4 Note A and Story 10.10 NFR5 verdict both rationalised real defects as deferrals; both were caught externally. **Self-review never closes a PARTIAL on its own** — a second LLM pass or project-lead read must explicitly approve or reclassify. Action Item #5.

3. **Inventory grep is a leaf-finder, not a dependency-finder.** Story 11.1's grep on `JP w_ABORT_cf` missed `asm_die`'s indirect callers. Migration inventory work should grep on the helper too. Action Item #6.

4. **EXX-hygiene at kernel-internal entries is convention masquerading as obvious.** Story 11.5 cycled through 4 EXX adds + 2 EXX removes before settling. The convention was implicit until 11.5's review forced it inline. Future Epic-12/13 stories must re-verify per site. Action Item #7.

5. **Audit-only stories tempt zero-finding self-review most strongly, and need full adversarial discipline.** Story 11.8's self-review found 5 findings; fresh-context found 6 more. Audit-only ≠ review-light. Carry-forward with #1.

6. **AC composition errors are a different bug class than AC-trace errors.** Three AC composition errors landed inside Epic 11 (DROP not underflowing with 3 cells; `'` parse-time semantics; CONSTANT-inside-`:` failing at compile time). The AC-trace-check should be extended to *executable validation* of the AC's literal form. Action Item #4.

7. **Mid-epic bug-fix interludes work well as separate stories.** Story 11.4.1 was clean — its own story file, ACs, review. The defect was tracked in `sprint-status.yaml` with a hard ordering constraint to land before Story 11.7. Reusable pattern for any future epic that surfaces a latent design flaw mid-flight.

8. **Net-new exception subsystem in +653 bytes (+3.9%) with zero regressions across 661+126 tests is the strong-form delivery.** The Phase-2 NFR4 envelope (no per-epic net-negative) was set up exactly so this kind of net-new mechanism could ship without ROM-shrink heroics. Epics 12 and 13 are the planned shrink epics — but see Significant Discovery #1 below.

## 🚨 SIGNIFICANT DISCOVERIES — EPIC UPDATE REQUIRED 🚨

**Two discoveries flip the verdict from Epic 10's "no epic-update required" to "Epic 12 needs redrafting" + "debt-cleanup interlude needed first."**

### Discovery 1 — Epic 12 is out of date

Epic 12 (`epics.md:925`+, sprint-status `12-1..12-7`) was drafted assuming a `ASSEMBLER.FTH` migration with auto-loading assembler words inside `CODE`/`END-CODE`. That plan was **rolled back on 2026-04-20** per memory `project_assembler_keep_assembly.md` ("Do not re-propose migrating src/assembler.asm to ASSEMBLER.FTH; decided 2026-04-20"). Story 12.6 ("ASSEMBLER wordlist + auto-activation in CODE/END-CODE") and the framing of Stories 12.1–12.5 have not been refreshed to match.

**Consequence:** Epic 12 stories assume a kernel-resident ASSEMBLER wordlist *populated by* the existing in-kernel opcode words, but several of the dependency comments and the Story 12.6 ACs still reference the old "auto-activation" plan. The `asm_hash_dispatch_hack` debt item (memory `project_asm_hash_dispatch_hack.md`) was scheduled for retirement *in* Story 12.6 — that retirement plan is invalid as-written.

**Required action before Epic 12 dev:** redraft Epic 12 epic-spec sections (12.1–12.7) to match the kept-in-assembly decision. This is an `edit-prd` / `edit-epic` exercise, not an Epic 12 story — needs to land before Story 12.1's `create-story` runs.

### Discovery 2 — Hardware-only crash class on real MicroBeast (not in iz-cpm)

Project lead 2026-04-27: AntForth crashes *a lot* on real MicroBeast hardware but never under the iz-cpm emulator. Manifests as hard reboot (banner reappears mid-line) or weird BDOS error (e.g., `error -3085` — not a defined THROW code) often followed by a `Bdos Err On B: Bad Sector` chain. Pattern observation: crashes happen during *print* operations.

**Empirical evidence (2026-04-27 hardware smoke transcript `~/Downloads/bestialitty-20260427-120911.bin`):**

1. `1 2 3 ' ABORT CATCH . . . .` — printed `-1 ` then **the MicroBeast soft-rebooted mid-line** (banner reappeared). Re-typed line then succeeded with `-1 3 2 1  ok`.
2. After a `THIS-DOES-NOT-EXIST` line later in the session, output produced `error -3085` (impossible — no THROW code -3085 exists in the table; BC must have held garbage that `print_throw_description` then drove through its lookup), followed by a `Bdos Err On B: Bad Sector` chain that eventually rebooted.
3. Multiple finger-trouble retypes also crashed; the user's finding is that the manifestation is reliably tied to print operations.

**Hypothesis space (to be audited):**
- **Shadow-register corruption around BDOS:** we assume BDOS preserves the shadow set; the CP/M BDOS contract is implementation-defined for the shadow set. Real BDOS implementations vary.
- **IX/IY preservation across BDOS:** same shape — assumed but not contractually guaranteed by all CP/M implementations.
- **Stack discipline during multi-cell printing:** `. . . .` does push/pop sequences, and the crash on `1 2 3 ' ABORT CATCH . . . .` is suspicious — that's also the i*x preservation path Story 11.4.1 redesigned.
- **UserArea / system area collisions:** are we writing past `pic_buf` or below `s0`? CCD-1 frame growth?
- **Stack-overflow guard absence (Epic 11 challenge #5):** no `-3` guard; an unguarded SP excursion past `s0` would clobber whatever lives below.
- **Real-MicroBeast firmware bug:** possible — but the user wants a thorough audit of antforth code first before involving the maintainer.

**Required action:** dedicated audit story — review every shadow-register / IX / IY / stack interaction with BDOS calls; cross-reference with real-MicroBeast BDOS contract; reproduce on hardware with diagnostic instrumentation; deliver verdict (antforth bug, hardware/firmware bug, or shared-fault) before approaching the MicroBeast maintainer.

### Recommendation: insert a debt-cleanup interlude epic before Epic 12

The combination of (a) accumulated technical debt (now 6 items, none retired by Epic 11), (b) Epic 12 being out of date, and (c) the hardware-only crash class needing audit, motivates a **debt-cleanup interlude epic (proposed: Epic 11.5 — "Stabilisation, hardware audit, and Epic-12 redraft preparation")** before Epic 12 starts. The interlude collects the deferred work into one stabilisation pass so it doesn't snowball into Epic 12's scope.

**Proposed Epic 11.5 stories (illustrative — pending `create-prd` / `create-epics` work):**

- **11.5.1 — Real-MicroBeast hardware crash audit.** Reproduce on hardware; instrument; isolate shadow-set / IX / IY / stack / UserArea root cause; deliver verdict (antforth defect vs. firmware vs. shared). If antforth, fix; if firmware, prepare reproducer for MicroBeast maintainer.
- **11.5.2 — Stack-overflow `-3 THROW` guard.** Wire a guard at compile-time helpers that grow data stack (`PUSH BC` paths under DEFCODE), or at the `do_underflow_error` analog point. Closes Story 11.8 NFR6 documented gap.
- **11.5.3 — `(` / EVALUATE source-frame fix.** Story 11.6 F8 deferral. Closes the EVALUATE-bounded parse defect; unlocks the deferred caught-form coverage for asm-error THROW codes (Story 11.5 D2).
- **11.5.4 — `print_throw_description` table-walk hardening.** Bound the linear walk; consider 16-bit `ADD HL,A` form if the table grows past ~40 entries. Cold path; defensive only.
- **11.5.5 — Epic 12 redraft.** Update `epics.md` Epic 12 sections + sprint-status story keys to match the 2026-04-20 "keep assembler in assembly" decision; rewrite Story 12.6 to land the ASSEMBLER wordlist by populating from kernel-resident words (no `.FTH` migration); update `asm_hash_dispatch_hack` debt-retirement plan accordingly.
- **11.5.6 — `-271` semantic split (Story 11.6 F4).** Allocate `-272` and split `+D` displacement vs `BIT,` bit-number range errors. Optional — judgment call on whether the diagnostic-locality gain justifies the EQU + table churn.
