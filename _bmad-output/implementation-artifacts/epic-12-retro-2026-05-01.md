## Successes

- **Search-Order subsystem ships antforth from "Forth toy" to "ANS-shaped Forth library."** Pre-Epic-12 antforth had one wordlist, period. Epic 12 delivers WORDLIST · SEARCH-WORDLIST · GET-ORDER · SET-ORDER · ONLY · GET-CURRENT · SET-CURRENT · DEFINITIONS · FORTH-WORDLIST — the full ANS §16.6.1 wordset plus the §16.6.2 ONLY extension. **Project lead 2026-05-01:** *"wordlist support makes antforth a much more rounded mature offering."* This is the headline.

- **Shared-helper extraction from Story 12.2 paid back across the rest of the epic.** `search_wid_for_name` factored from FIND in 12.2 was reused **unchanged** in 12.3's search-order walk and 12.4's per-wordlist lookup. **Fifth epic with `feedback_design_upfront.md` paying back** — design candidate helpers spanning ≥2 stories deliberately, not opportunistically.

- **Per-definition saved-wid pattern (Story 12.4) is genuinely elegant.** Three rollback slots (`bh_wid` for build_header, `colon_saved_wid` for `:`, `asm_saved_wid` for assembler) give each compilation path independent state without a global wid stack and without graph snapshotting. This pattern generalises directly to Epic 13's INCLUDE source-frame nesting (Story 13.4 E13-D2's 10-byte frame layout).

- **Adversarial review caught the MARKER-on-foreign-wid corruption.** Story 12.4's H1 fix: if `MARKER`'s header lives in a foreign wordlist and snapshot/restore reads `bh_wid != forth_wordlist`, FORTH-WORDLIST's bucket array gets corrupted on restore. This is silent data corruption — the kind of bug that only surfaces with very specific MARKER usage patterns. Test 837 (T-MARKER-XWID-EXEC) added as regression probe. **Adversarial review remains the right vehicle for cross-cutting invariants.**

- **Story 12.5 ONLY shipped clean — idempotence-by-construction.** Stateless writes to two USER fields make `ONLY ONLY` trivially identical to `ONLY`. ONLY preserves BC (no TOS touch). ONLY does NOT touch `current_wordlist` (common naïve-implementation defect avoided by design). Tests verify all three properties (T-ONLY-IDEMPOTENT, T-ONLY-PRESERVES-CURRENT, T-ONLY-TOS-PRESERVES).

- **CCD-4 close-out gate hit all verdicts:** NFR2 (multi-vocab lookup ≤3% relative cost · PASS), NFR4 (ROM trajectory · PASS), NFR9 (zero regression · PASS), NFR17/CCD-3 (citations · PASS), FR31 (CODE backward-compat byte-identical · PASS), FR45/FR46 (full regression · PASS), MVP (hardware smoke · PASS). v1.12 release-ready.

## Challenges

### 1. Story 12.3 +362B vs +200B envelope — significant overrun

**+162B / +81% over the upper budget bound.** UserArea extension (34B) + cold-start LDIR→DJNZ switch + FIND search-order walk + THROW -49 infrastructure. Story 12.3 was the structural keystone — once it landed, 12.4 / 12.5 came in close to envelope. Project-lead framing 2026-05-01: *"code size overages are lamentable, but if there's a tight initial budget then we are at least striving not to be profligate."* **This reframes Action Item #3 (real-byte-count estimation): the discipline matters more than the gate.** A tight budget overshot by a real story is doing useful work; a slack budget hit on the nose is meaningless. See Lesson 12-C.

### 2. DEFCODE header overhead understated in spec budgets

Stories 12.2, 12.4, 12.5 — `hash_link` (2B) + `count_flags` (1B) + name (N B) + JP DOCOL (3B for DEFWORDs) per header was not itemised in the rough budgets. Each 11-character word ≈ 14B of header alone. Resolution: per-component reconciliation in story-close audit, not refactor. **Process improvement:** future story specs should budget header overhead explicitly (sum of `(hash_link + count + namelen + threading_prologue)` per word) rather than rolling it into a "feature implementation" line.

### 3. TIB 128-byte truncation discovered in 12.4, carried as known idiom in 12.5

Long REPL test lines silently truncate mid-word, no error, no warning. Worked around by splitting test lines into multiple `printf %s\r\n` arguments. **Project-lead direction 2026-05-01:** TIB stays at 128B for now (possible future adjustment); document the limit in test-author guidance so anyone writing fresh tests knows. **See Action Item A1 + Lesson 12-D.**

### 4. AC line-number drift recurring (carry from prior epics)

Stories 12.x AC drafts repeatedly required re-anchoring against current HEAD line numbers. Same root cause as Epic 11 / 11.5 challenges. **No new action required** — Epic 11.5 retro Action Item #6 ("inventory grep covers helpers, not just leaves") implicitly mandates grep-as-source-of-truth. The discipline holds; AC re-anchoring is now routine in dev-pass kickoff.

### 5. NFR2 verdict was analytic, not measured

No `make bench` infrastructure exists; T-state accounting for the NFR2 multi-vocab lookup overhead was per-edit analytic walk. Verdict is sound (~30 T-states slot-0 hit overhead, ≤3% relative on ~1000+ T-state FIND path), but a measurement-backed verdict would be stronger. Not surfaced as urgent — Phase-2 is the wrong time to invest in benchmarking infrastructure when the analytic verdict is conservative. **Carry-forward opportunity:** `make bench` infrastructure if/when ROM pressure or perf-claim disputes surface.

## Key Insights

1. **Lesson 12-A — Shared helpers extracted in early stories pay back across the rest of the epic.** `search_wid_for_name` factored from FIND in Story 12.2 was reused unchanged in 12.3+. Fifth epic of `feedback_design_upfront.md` paying back. **How to apply:** in early-epic story specs, identify candidate helpers spanning ≥2 stories; extract them deliberately, not opportunistically.

2. **Lesson 12-B — Per-definition saved-state slots beat global rollback stacks.** Story 12.4's `bh_wid` / `colon_saved_wid` / `asm_saved_wid` per-site discipline keeps each compilation path's rollback independent. **How to apply:** when a new system-wide variable is introduced (next: SOURCE-ID frames in Epic 13.4), prefer per-site save/restore slots over a global stack.

3. **Lesson 12-C — Tight per-story byte budgets ratchet even when overshot.** The discipline matters more than the gate. **Why:** project lead 2026-05-01 — *"if there's a tight initial budget then we are at least striving not to be profligate."* **How to apply:** keep per-story budgets tight; treat overshoots as sizing-data not failures; don't relax the gate after a miss. Reframes Epic 11.5 retro Action Item #3 from a hard ±5% gate to a ratchet-with-honest-variance-tracking.

4. **Lesson 12-D — TIB 128-byte limit silently truncates long REPL test lines mid-word.** Discovered Story 12.4; carried as known idiom in 12.5. **How to apply:** test authors split long lines into multiple `printf %s\r\n` arguments; documented in test-author guidance (see Action Item A1).

5. **Adversarial review remains the structural-defect catcher.** Story 12.4's MARKER-on-foreign-wid H1 fix is the canonical example — neither dev pass nor design-upfront caught it; review's "what could go wrong with cross-cutting invariants?" question surfaced it. **Ninth consecutive epic where review surfaces ≥1 finding per story.**

6. **Standards citation discipline holds.** Every word shipped in Epic 12 cites its ANS section inline (§16.6.1 for the core wordset, §16.6.2.1965 for ONLY, §9.3.5 for THROW -49). NFR17/CCD-3 audit clean. Pattern from Epic 9 / 10 / 11 / 11.5 carrying through.
